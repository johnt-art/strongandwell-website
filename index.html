Frequently asked questions

How much does the FDN approach cost?

Each plan is tailored to the individual's needs/wants. We will meet for a discovery call to see how I may assist you and will discuss program details once we decide what route best serves you.

Do you have to run functional screenings?

There are options which can start with basic lifestyle and food choice changes, but long-term healing will come from screenings which speak to your own personal needs. The most success comes from running the functional screenings.

Do you accept HSA/FSA?

IF HSA is a debit style card, you can usually use it towards screenings and/or paying for your programs. FSA is an individual case and will need to be followed up with by the client, not Strong & Well.

What if I do not see the results I expect? Is there a refund?

As stated above, this is an opt-in model of self-care. What results you obtain is up to your level of commitment not just to a short-term quick fix, but to long term healing. This takes time, commitment, patience and grace. FDN is NOT a quick fix and there can be amazing positive steps, but no one's journey is the same. There are no guarantees. Strong & Well will provide the best possible approach for your needs/wants/constraints, etc., but beyond that it's up to you to implement and to follow through.

How is this different from my normal doctor or standard of care?

FDN approach does not "diagnose" anything. We simply provide accurate assessments of functional screenings along with detailed intake forms. We combine these things together and formulate a plan of action for long term healing opportunities. We are looking upstream from your "signs/symptoms" so that your body can heal itself instead of providing a symptom-based band aid in the form of a pharmaceutical agent or surgery.
